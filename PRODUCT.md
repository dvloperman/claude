# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript; GitHub Pages, no build step or private backend.

## Users

Developers and technical decision-makers evaluating coding agents, with enough context to value evidence, reproducibility, and source attribution.

## Product Purpose

AELTRIX TEAM’s independent editorial and technical showcase explains Claude Code’s documented capabilities and compares them with OpenAI Codex using sourced product facts and transparent evaluation methodology. Success means visitors can understand the workflow, inspect the sources, and distinguish product documentation from measured coding performance.

## Positioning

The site is an independent technical publication and showcase, not an Anthropic or OpenAI property. It presents Claude Code as the subject while refusing to invent comparative wins or benchmark results.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a static, responsive GitHub Pages site. They can inspect an illustrative coding workflow, switch between product and performance comparison views, and follow source links.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site has no private backend and no dynamic benchmark data feed.
- Product claims must cite official Anthropic or OpenAI documentation where possible.
- Performance claims must distinguish source-reported results from independent controlled evaluations.
- No benchmark scores, quotations, or test outcomes may be fabricated.
- A real head-to-head performance run has not been conducted for this release; the site states this explicitly and presents a proposed protocol only.
- The workflow visualization is illustrative and must not be represented as a recorded Claude Code session.

## Brand Commitments

- Organization: AELTRIX TEAM.
- Created, designed, and developed by: Mr.R3B.
- Independent editorial and technical showcase; not officially affiliated with Anthropic or OpenAI.
- Approved creative direction: The Workbench, with the instrument-lit dark palette and evidence-led editorial architecture.

## Evidence on Hand

First-party Anthropic and OpenAI documentation and published research links are recorded in the site’s source ledger. No site-generated head-to-head benchmark data or measured outcomes are available.

## Product Principles

- Demonstrate the process; do not substitute hype for evidence.
- Attribute claims to their sources and dates.
- Keep documented capabilities separate from measured performance.
- Make limitations and unknowns visible.
- Remain independent in ownership and presentation.
